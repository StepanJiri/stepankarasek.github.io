A few days ago, Apple announced new games coming to Apple Arcade, and I think they are pretty good.

**Plants vs. Zombies**: This is some kind of remaster/remake. A year ago I saw a [review](https://youtu.be/zik-Cx7oxQE?is=yKRuKfWMNVRzPFs1 "Plants vs Zombies Is a Complete Mess on Switch 2") and it didn't look good.Let's hope Apple has checked the quality of the game.

**Solitaire(ish)**: Apple proudly said that the publisher is the same as for the absolutely amazing game Balatro. Let's see if it's any good. It's a card game.

**Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown+**: This is the only new game with a plus at the end, because the paid version is on the App Store. It's the second Ubisoft game on this list. I heard a lot of good things about this game when it came out. I'm looking forward to playing it too.

**LEGO DUPLO Playtime**: I can't wait to play this.

Apple Arcade games are good, and there 
are more and more of them. Loving it. It's a very underrated Apple service, and I like that Netflix is competing with Arcade.
