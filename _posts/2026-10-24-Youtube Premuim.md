If you have YouTube Premium and are offline with a downloaded video, you can't like the video. You can't open the full description, so if you watch a [concert](https://youtu.be/KDMX2eE7xG8?is=ErKZtmFHFaulhPiC), you can't see the timestamps. You can't get the URL for the video. You can't download specific videos at different resolutions. And surely more. The UI is all over the place. The download indicator is on the same line as the name of the channel. Why? YouTube offers automatic downloads. It downloads Shorts automatically.

The reason I think it is stupid not to offer likes in offline mode is that the phone has memory, so you can just upload the information later. It is similar with the URL: you can just save the video URL while downloading the video.

Competition is needed for YouTube. The problem is that YouTube is not a gatekeeper. Anyone can offer videos online, and they do, but YouTube is one big platform where everyone is. I would love to see an open protocol for videos used more. There is PeerTube, which is cool, but almost no one is there.

I am just glad that this is my free month trial.
