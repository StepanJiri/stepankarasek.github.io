You can react with emoji to a message in most modern messaging apps. But the way you do it varies from app to app. So mental weight varies by app. 

You can touch and hold on a message to copy the text, save it, etc., but you can also react to the message with emoji. This is how it works on most messaging platforms. 

But there is another way to do it. On Instagram, you can double-tap on a message to react with a preset emoji right away. On the Apple Messages app or WhatsApp it doesn’t work that way. Yes, you get a menu to select a reaction, but the reaction is not applied right away, and you don’t get a menu for copying text, etc., only the reaction panel. 

This might be a small difference for most people. But for me, this is a very big difference. 

Instagram offers a simple way to react. I would guess that the variety of emoji is much smaller than on Messages or WhatsApp just because Instagram offers this double-tap way. 

WhatsApp and Instagram are both products of Meta. But they both have a different set of customers. WhatsApp is more broad. Everyone has it. Instagram, on the other hand, has a “younger” audience. My school messaging group is on Instagram. 

On Instagram the double-tap feels like "mark as read". If someone sends you a reel and you don’t at least double-tap it, the other person thinks you did not see the reel. 

The heart symbol loses its meaning if it's sent many times a day. But that is on the side. 

This shows how different apps (companies) treat their users. It also shows how one little thing can change the behaviour of users.
