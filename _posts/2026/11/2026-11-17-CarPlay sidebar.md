iOS 26 and 27 made the left vertical bar with information like time, cellular, recent apps, and the Home button into a floating island made of Liquid Glass. Let's call that Sidebar.

![Inline Image Description]({{ 'assets/images/CarPlay Sidebar/New_CarPlay.jpg' | relative_url }})
And I don't like that. Every single element on the right is its own island too, and I like that. So why do I hate the sidebar? Because, and this is very important, IT DOES NOT CHANGE shape.

***

Let me explain. In the image above, all the right islands like the map, directions, flight details, and now playing change is only there for some active reason. If no flight is being tracked, then there is no flight information. If there are no directions, then there is no direction. So they change depending on the information that CarPlay determines is important, like an upcoming calendar event or a recommended destination.

But the Sidebar on the left is not changing. I know I am saying it again, but just looking at it, if I had no knowledge about how this software behaves, I would say it can change. The things on the right look like they can change by what they show. They don't show permanent information. Like the flight information; the plane is going to touch the ground eventually. Time and cellular information are not going to end. So do not make look like it can change. 

And it actually used to be like that. The image below shows the vertical bar filled edge to edge, except on the right, of course.

![Inline Image Description]({{ 'assets/images/CarPlay Sidebar/Old_CarPlay.png' | relative_url }})

Liquid Glass is, for me, more liquid than glass. Across iOS and iPadOS I think they do a good job. MacOS is kind of sad, but that is not important. When something is made of the material Liquid Glass, I think it should move. 

***

MacOS 26 and iPadOS 26 introduce a similar thing to the floating sidebar. It looked similar to the CarPlay Sidebar, but in MacOS 27 and iPadOS 27 it is no longer there. And that is good. Just hope that Apple realised the same thing here. 

And BTW, we would have more horizontal space if they would give me one less island and the padding from the left.
