I found a very interesting [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y227RF0smAg).

It is an interview with Craig Federighi about the iPhone Duo. The moment I saw the thumbnail on my home page, I knew I wanted to watch it. Until then, I had not heard Federighi speak about the iPhone Duo.

The host had amazing questions. I have never seen Federighi talk about details like this before, not even on The Talk Show with John Gruber. Most of the time, Federighi speaks in detail about technical implementation, like a new API, a private way to offer contact fill-in to your app without giving it access to the person's contacts, etc.

Here Federighi speaks about very specific design decisions: the way to go back on iOS, and the way Apple designed the animation that plays every time you open the Duo.

The host is a person named Donald Decodes. I have not seen him anywhere else before, and after looking at his channel, it appears to me that he is new to making videos. Good job.
