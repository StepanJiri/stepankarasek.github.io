---
title: "Calculator off the screen"
---

In macOS Golden Gate the default Apple calculator app has sidebar menu that shows history. When the sidebar is enable the main view of calculation moves right and resizes the width of the windows to make new space on the left for the history. 

While I was playing with the app. I was able to move the window to left by hiding are showing the sidebar by mouse. Any sidebar can be hidden to resized by hovering over the edge of the sidebar and grading left to hide or left to make the width of the sidebar bigger.
