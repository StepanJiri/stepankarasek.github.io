In Apple’s 2026 Environmental Progress [Report](https://www.apple.com/environment/pdf/Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2026.pdf), one of the charts is rendered wrongly (page 5). The report says Apple is 43 percent of the way to its goal of increasing supplier participation in the Supplier Clean Water Program, but the progress bar next to it is filled to well over 80 percent.
