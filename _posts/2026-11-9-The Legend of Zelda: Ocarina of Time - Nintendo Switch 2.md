I am not excited about the *Ocarina of Time* remake at all. Don't get me wrong, I love Zelda games.

My first big game was *Breath of the Wild* in December 2017. I loved it and still do. The game is built around exploration, and at that time I didn't know English very well, so the experience of exploring felt even stronger. I remember to this day when I realized that I could unlock more hearts or stamina by total chance.

*Tears of the Kingdom* is an even better game in my opinion. The exploration went to a whole new level, and it's still the best game I have ever played. I even enjoyed *Echoes of Wisdom*. These games have the appeal of having no boundaries. You can finish any challenge in any way you want.

But I have never finished any other Zelda game. I tried a few on Switch Online, like *The Wind Waker*, *The Minish Cap*, the original *Zelda* and *Zelda II*. They are just too strict and linear for me.

That brings me to the point. I also tried *Ocarina of Time*. The controls on the Nintendo Switch Online version were terrible. The Nintendo 64 controller had only one stick, so the camera doesn't work the way it does in modern games. I just couldn't figure out how to play it. I never got past Kokiri Forest. If I ever finish it, I want to play the original, with the original controller and graphics. So I don't feel the need to play the new remake.
