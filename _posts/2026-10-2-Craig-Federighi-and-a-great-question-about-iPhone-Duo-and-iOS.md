I found a very interesting [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y227RF0smAg)

It is an interview with Craig Federighi about the iPhone Duo. The moment I saw the thumbnail on my home page, I knew I wanted to see it. To that point, I had not yet seen Federighi speak about the iPhone Duo. 

The host had amazing questions. I have never seen Federighi speak about details like in this video before. Not even The Talk Show with John Gruber. Here Federighi speaks about very specific design decisions. The way to go back on iOS or the way Apple designed the animation that plays every time you open Duo.

The host is a person named Donald Decodes. I have not seen him anywhere else before. And after looking at his channel it appears to me that he is new to making videos. Good job
