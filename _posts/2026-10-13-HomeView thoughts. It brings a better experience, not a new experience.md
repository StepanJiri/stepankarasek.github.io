First, I like the name. It is for a home, it has a display, and it sees. So I am pretty satisfied with Apple's names recently: iPhone Duo, MacBook Neo and now HomeView. Cool.

It should have Face ID. Relying on camera recognition and nearby devices is not cool. But maybe the LiDAR sensor doesn't have a long enough range. And the price may be a reason too.

In this three-minute video by Apple, there is not a single thing done on HomeView that couldn't be done on an iPhone or even an Apple Watch. Yes, it has a bigger display, so more people can look at it than at the small Apple Watch, and especially the camera view. So it brings a better experience, not a new experience.

In the same video I did not see a single Apple Watch, iPhone, iPad, Mac, etc. I wonder why. Okay, an AirTag was there.

1:07, still in the same video. The mother is looking for her keys while the father is on a FaceTime call on the HomeView. So the mother cannot use the HomeView (time for a personal computer?), but it just so happens that the father finishes the call and now the HomeView is free. Ad magic.

One more little thing: during the FaceTime call, the ellipsis is not a prominent Liquid Glass button, so it doesn’t have a background colour, and because the button is big, you can see everything morphing under the blur. I wonder if there is a setting for the Liquid Glass look.

Using iMessage on this thing is crazy and pointless. I understand Siri prompting and sending messages, but viewing the actual message thread on the screen, I don't see a world where it would be useful. I guess if you don't have your phone on you and you want to type.

They have a table made just for the cable to get through and plug in the HomeView. I am so happy that it doesn't have a fixed USB-C cable like the HomePod mini does.
