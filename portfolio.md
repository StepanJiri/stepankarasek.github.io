## Games
You can see and play all of my video games on [itch.io](https://jirka-karasek.itch.io/). With game design, I love to think similar like one very specific video game [company](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/index.html) from Japan. 

I very strongly believe that story and visuals are not as important as the game mechanics. Video games are not paintings, movies or books. It is an interactive medium and I want to make as much advantage of it as possible.

I work in Unreal Engine and use Blueprints.

### Big Games
My two big project Rubicon and Shift are made with care for game design. 

[**Shift**](https://jirka-karasek.itch.io/shift) is my first finished solo video game. It is 2D platformer where player can teleport to an orb. But the orbs come in different styles, one stays on one place, one switches places instantly, one constantly moves, and the last one player control by moving around. Here is [gameplay](https://youtu.be/vPJqZz_C4yU) 

[**Rubicon**](https://jirka-karasek.itch.io/rubicon) is my biggest game to this point. It is a metroidvania game. Every single upgrade changes and influences the other. It is a first-person shooter. With Rubicon, I won first position at CTRL + GAME Ostrava 2025.
