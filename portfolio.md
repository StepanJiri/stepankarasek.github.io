## Games  
You can see and play all of my video games on [itch.io](https://jirka-karasek.itch.io/). With game design, I love to think similarly to one very specific video game [company](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/index.html) from Japan.   
  
I very strongly believe that story and visuals are not as important as the game mechanics. Video games are not paintings, movies or books. It is an interactive medium and I want to make as much advantage of it as possible.  
  
My first ever experience with a GUI game engine in some form was the Warcraft 3 map editor. Later, Minecraft with command blocks, then Fortnite Creative and from there to [Blender](https://www.blender.org) and [Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/unreal-engine-5).  
  
In Unreal Engine, I code with blueprints, use Niagara effects and much more. In Blender, I make models and texture them.   
  
Very lightly used Godot.   
  
### Big Games  
My two big projects, Rubicon and Shift, are made with care for game design.   
  
[Shift](https://jirka-karasek.itch.io/shift) is my first finished solo video game. It is a 2D platformer where the player can teleport to an orb. But the orbs come in different styles: one stays in one place, one switches places instantly, one constantly moves, and the last one the player controls by moving around. Here is the [gameplay](https://youtu.be/vPJqZz_C4yU)  
  
  
**[Rubicon](https://jirka-karasek.itch.io/rubicon)** is my biggest game to this point. It is a metroidvania game. Every single upgrade changes and influences the others. It is a first-person shooter. With Rubicon, I won first position at CTRL + GAME Ostrava 2025.  
  
### Game Jams   
  
I attended game jams from [Game Devs Ostrava](https://gameova.cz/) in early and late 2025 and early and late 2026. It was over a weekend and most of the time I was the programmer. It was a team of four and as a team we came up with the idea.   
  
My most favourite project from a game jam is [free  mind](https://jirka-karasek.itch.io/free-mind) (small f and two spaces between words is not a typo) It is a platformer with a focus on escape. From the early 2026 game jam.  
  
One time I attended [GMTK Game Jam 2025](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2025). I was alone and made two games. It was very stressful. Looking back at this, I don’t like these two games.
