# Games
You can see and play all of my video games on [itch.io](https://jirka-karasek.itch.io/). With game design, I love to think similar like one very specific video game [company](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/index.html) from Japan. I very strongly believe that story and visuals are not as important as the game mechanics. Video games are not paintings, movies or books. It is an interactive medium and I want to make as much advantage of it as possible.

I work in Unreal Engine and use Blueprints. 

I have visited an over four game jam. It was over weekend and most of the time I was the programer. It was team of four and as team we come up with the idea. My most favourite project from game jam is free  mind (two spaces between words and small f is not a type.)
